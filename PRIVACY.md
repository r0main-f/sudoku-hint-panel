# Privacy policy — Sudoku Hint Panel

*Last updated: 27 October 2026*

Sudoku Hint Panel does **not** collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.

**What it reads.** On sudoku.com pages only, the extension reads the current puzzle: the game state the site already keeps in your browser's local storage, and the puzzle the site downloads from its own server when a new game starts. This is used solely to compute hints inside your browser.

**What it stores.** Only panel settings, in the page's local storage on your device: the panel's position, whether it is collapsed, and whether you dismissed the welcome note. Clearing sudoku.com's site data removes them.

**What it sends.** Nothing. The extension makes no network requests of its own, includes no analytics, and loads no remote code.

**Permissions.** It runs only on `https://sudoku.com/*` and `https://www.sudoku.com/*` and requests no other browser permissions.

**Contact.** Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/r0main-f/sudoku-hint-panel/issues.

Sudoku Hint Panel is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Easybrain or sudoku.com.
