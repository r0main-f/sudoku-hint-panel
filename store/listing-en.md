## Description

Sudoku Hint Panel is a learning companion for the puzzles on sudoku.com. It never solves the grid for you unless you ask: it tells you whether a step is available, which technique it uses and where it is — at the level of help you choose, from just filling your notes to the full reasoning of the next move.

THE PANEL, FROM THE LEAST HELP TO THE MOST
1 · Notes — no answers given
• ✎ Candidates fills in every empty cell's pencil notes.
• ✎ Optimized fills them in already narrowed by every pair, triple, pointing and chain erasure.

2 · Step by step assist — one hint at a time
• 🧭 Assist guides you through the grid, always with the easiest hint left: first where to look, then what (the technique and its cells), then why (the reasoning drawn on the grid). Apply it and the next one appears; Skip jumps ahead.
• ☝ ONE single shows a single next move and keeps it until you fill the cell.

3 · Fill in — answers typed for you
• ⚡ Fill singles types the digit into every cell where only one fits.
• 🏁 Finish grid types the whole solution when you're stuck for good (click twice to confirm).

4 · Advanced — all hints at once (folded until you open it)
• How many hints of each technique exist right now; open one to see them on the grid, and hover a hint to see why it works and which earlier hints it relies on.

TECHNIQUES, FROM THE SIMPLEST
• Single option — a cell where only one digit fits, or a digit with only one place left in a row, column or box. Hover a green cell to see why: blue circles mark the digits that rule out the rest, grey × the cells that can't take it.
• Pointing — a box's candidates for a digit all in one row or column (or the other way round): that digit goes from the rest of the line (or box).
• Pairs & triples — naked (2 cells sharing the same 2 candidates, or 3 sharing 3) and hidden (2 digits that fit only in the same 2 cells, or 3 in 3).
• Chains — where a digit has only two places, linked cells alternate between two colours, and one colour holds all of that digit.
• Expert techniques, folded away until you want them: quads, X-wing & fish (swordfish, jellyfish), wings (XY, XYZ, W), unique rectangles, long chains (X- and XY-chains) and forcing chains.
A harder technique is only used when everything simpler finds nothing, so hints stay as easy as the grid allows. Hints that only appear once earlier ones are applied are found too, tagged "step 2, step 3…".

GOOD TO KNOW
• Works on Classic and Killer games, the daily challenge and special editions (e.g. Storybook).
• Updates live as you play, works on a brand-new grid before your first move, never draws over a site pop-up, and goes quiet when no game is on screen.
• If one of your notes rules out the digit that really goes there, the panel tells you and shows where, instead of giving a wrong hint.
• Light, movable panel; green is used only where a single digit fits.

FAIR PLAY
Regular games on sudoku.com also count for its tournaments and leaderboards. Hints give you an advantage other players don't have there — use them to learn, and play ranked games on your own.

PRIVACY
No data is collected, stored remotely or sent anywhere. The extension runs only on sudoku.com pages, reads the game state the site keeps in your own browser, and has no extra permissions.
