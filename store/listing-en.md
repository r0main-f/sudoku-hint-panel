## Description

Sudoku Hint Panel is a learning companion for the puzzles on sudoku.com. It never solves the grid for you unless you ask: it tells you whether a step is available, which technique it uses and where it is — at the level of help you choose.

LEARN AT YOUR OWN LEVEL
• Just the counts: the panel shows how many single options, pairs & triples, pointing and chain hints exist right now. Nothing is drawn on the grid until you open a category.
• One technique: open a category to see its hints faintly on the grid.
• One hint: "☝ ONE single" shows a single next move, easiest first, and keeps it until you fill the cell.
• Step by step assist: "🧭 Assist" guides you one hint at a time, from the simplest technique to the most complex — first where to look, then what, then why. Apply it and the next one appears; Skip jumps ahead.
• The reasoning: with a category on, hover one of its hints on the grid to see it in full, with the earlier hints it relies on — so next time you can find it yourself.
• Why it's a single, drawn on the grid: hover a green cell — blue circles show the digits that rule out the rest, grey × the cells that can't take it, red only when an earlier hint was needed.

TECHNIQUES
• Single option — a cell where only one digit fits, or a digit with only one place left in a row, column or box.
• Pairs & triples — 2 cells sharing the same 2 candidates (or 3 sharing 3): those digits can be erased from the rest of the row, column or box. Hidden pairs & triples too: 2 digits that fit in only the same 2 cells (or 3 in 3), so those cells' other notes go.
• Pointing — when a box's candidates for a digit all sit in one row or column, that digit can be erased from the rest of the line; and the other way round, when a row's or column's candidates all sit in one box, from the rest of the box.
• Chains — when nothing simpler helps: where a digit has only two places in a row, column or box, one of them holds it. Linked together, these cells alternate between two colours, and one colour holds all of that digit. A colour that would put the digit twice in one unit is wrong, and a cell that sees both colours can't hold it.
• Chained hints — hints that only appear once earlier ones are applied are found too, tagged "step 2, step 3…".

PRACTICAL TOOLS
• ✎ Candidates fills in every empty cell's pencil notes.
• ✎ Optimized fills them in already narrowed by every pair, triple and pointing erasure, chained until nothing changes.
• ⚡ Fill singles types the digit into every single-option cell.
• 🏁 Finish grid types the solution into every empty cell when you're stuck for good (click twice to confirm).
• Works on Classic and Killer games, the daily challenge and special editions (e.g. Storybook).
• Updates live as you play, works on a brand-new grid before your first move, and never draws over a site pop-up.
• Light, movable panel; green is used only where a single digit fits.

FAIR PLAY
Regular games on sudoku.com also count for its tournaments and leaderboards. Hints give you an advantage other players don't have there — use them to learn, and play ranked games on your own.

PRIVACY
No data is collected, stored remotely or sent anywhere. The extension runs only on sudoku.com pages, reads the game state the site keeps in your own browser, and has no extra permissions.
